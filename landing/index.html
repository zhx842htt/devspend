FAQ

Does my data leave my machine?

No. devspend reads local files and computes everything locally. No cloud, no telemetry, no accounts.

How accurate are the costs?

Token costs are list-price estimates including cache pricing, overridable at ~/.devspend/prices.json. Calibrate before invoicing — subscription plans (Claude Max, Cursor) don't pay per-token rates.

Is this just another ccusage?

ccusage is a great free CLI for Claude Code stats. devspend starts there but is built for billing: project/client attribution and invoice-ready exports. Both can coexist.

When can I bill clients with it?

Project attribution works today. Client entities, invoice-grade exports and budget alerts are Phase 2 (Pro, one-time license). Join the waitlist to get early access.
